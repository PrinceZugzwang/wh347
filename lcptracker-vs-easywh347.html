If your contract says "submit in LCPtracker"

Then you still submit there; nothing here replaces it. Agencies that use LCPtracker often don't charge contractors for it. North Dakota DOT's FAQ, for example, says "There is no cost to the contractor for the use of this service", and lets you type the payroll in or upload LCPtracker's Excel template.

What you pay for is preparing the payroll: working out overtime, fringe and deductions, and checking rates. That's the job LCPcertified and EasyWH347 both do.
