Behind on certified payroll? Send us the payroll. We'll catch you up.

On a prevailing wage job, the prime can hold your progress payment until every week's certified payroll is in. Send the payroll exports you already have. We prepare each missing week, oldest first, check it against the wage determination, and send it to you to sign.

Send your payroll

Two weeks free, then $199 a month per project. Cancel any time.

What we need from you

  • Your payroll for each missing week: a Gusto, ADP, Paychex or QuickBooks export, a spreadsheet, or photos of the timesheets.
  • The project's name and, if you have it, the wage determination number from the contract.
  • Where you file: a PDF to the prime, LCPtracker, California DIR eCPR, or a state portal.

What you get back

  • A signed-ready WH-347 for every missing week, in order.
  • For California, the eCPR file for each week; for weeks with no work, the statement of non-performance.
  • A list of anything that doesn't match the wage determination, so you can fix it before the agency finds it.
