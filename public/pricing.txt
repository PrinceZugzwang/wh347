WHAT IT DOES
Fills in the U.S. Department of Labor's Form WH-347 (certified payroll for
Davis-Bacon and Related Acts jobs, January 2025 revision, OMB 1235-0008) and
produces a PDF ready to sign, with DOL's own Statement of Compliance page.
Type the hours or import a payroll export (CSV or Excel from Gusto, ADP,
Paychex, QuickBooks or any spreadsheet). Overtime (hours over 40 in the week
at 1.5x), fringe, gross and net pay are worked out, and pay is checked against
the wage determination rates you paste in.

LIMITS
- Federal WH-347 for Davis-Bacon and Related Acts work. State upload files:
  California and New York only so far.
- Overtime follows the federal weekly rule (over 40 hours). State daily
  overtime rules (for example California's over 8 hours a day) are not
  calculated.
- Does not run payroll, pay workers, withhold taxes or file tax forms. It
  prepares the certified payroll report from pay you have already calculated
  or typed in.
- Wage rate checks compare against the rates you paste in; county rate pages
  cover Minnesota and California.
- EasyWH347 is an independent product, not a government site. You sign the
  Statement of Compliance and remain responsible for what you file.
