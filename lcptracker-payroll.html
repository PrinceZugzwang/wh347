How to enter certified payroll in LCPtracker

If your agency or prime uses LCPtracker, you enter each week's certified payroll there instead of emailing a WH‑347. Here's what contractors do, step by step. Menus can differ a little between agencies.

Work out the week first, free

Get hours, overtime, fringe and net pay right on the WH‑347 form, then copy them into LCPtracker.

Before your first payroll

  1. Your prime contractor has to add you to the project in LCPtracker. You'll get login details by email.
  2. Sign in at lcptracker.net. Under Set Up, create your eSignature. It's separate from your password, and you need it to certify payroll.
  3. Under Set Up, add every employee who will work on the project. Enter only the last four digits of the Social Security number.
  4. Many agencies require a fringe benefit statement on file before you can certify. It's under the eDocuments tab, with fringe listed as hourly rates.

Four ways to enter a week

Enter records
Pick the week ending date, project and employee, add their classification, then enter rates, hours on this project, fringe contributions, deductions, check amount and check number. Rates carry forward to the next week.
Copy previous payroll
Copy last week for the same people, then change the week ending date, check numbers and hours.
Upload records
Download the spreadsheet template from the Payroll Records tab, fill it in following its Instructions tab, and upload it. LCPtracker accepts up to 200 payroll records per upload. Dates go in m/d/yyyy format.
Payroll software
Some payroll systems export a file LCPtracker accepts. Ask LCPtracker support whether yours does.

Certify the week

When every employee and classification for the week is in, certify the payroll with your eSignature. Payrolls on federally funded work are due weekly; the agency or prime may reject a week and send it back for corrections.

Where EasyWH347 fits

LCPtracker checks what you enter, but you still have to work out each worker's straight time, overtime, fringe and net pay first.
