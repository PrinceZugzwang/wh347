When DOL's form is all you need

  • One short job, a handful of workers, and you're happy to type each week from scratch.
  • Your contract asks for DOL's form exactly as DOL serves it.

When EasyWH347 saves you time

  • The job runs for months and the crew is mostly the same each week.
  • Your hours are already in Gusto, ADP, QuickBooks or a spreadsheet, and you'd rather not type them twice.
  • You want the week checked against the wage determination before you sign it.

Either way, keep every week

Certified payrolls have to be kept for three years after all the work on the prime contract is finished. Neither free form keeps them for you, so save each week's PDF. EasyWH347 Pro, in development, will keep every week by project.
