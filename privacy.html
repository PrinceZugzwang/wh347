Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Your payroll is never uploaded

Whether you type the payroll into the form or import a payroll file, it is handled by code running in your browser tab. The workers' names, ID numbers, hours and pay are not sent to us or to anyone else. Once the page has loaded you can switch off your internet connection and the tool still works, which is a simple way to confirm this yourself.

What your browser remembers

To save you typing next week, your browser keeps the report header (company, project and wage determination details), the crew you typed into the form (names, the last four digits of each ID, classifications, rates, deductions and hours), your column matching and any wage rates you pasted. Full Social Security numbers are never kept. This is stored in your browser's local storage on your computer only. "Clear the form" on the page, or clearing your browser data, removes it.

If you use Pro

Pro is the one part of EasyWH347 that stores payroll on a server, and only the weeks you choose to save by pressing "Save to my records". For each saved week we keep the report header, the workers' names, the last four digits of their IDs, classifications, hours, rates, deductions and pay, and the wage rates you entered. Full Social Security numbers are never sent. Records are kept in our database provider, Supabase, and only your signed-in account can read them. We keep them so you have them for the three years after the work ends that Davis-Bacon rules require; you can delete a week yourself, and we delete your account and records on request to support@easywh347.com. To sign in we keep your email address. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

If you use done-for-you filing

To prepare and file your certified payroll for you, we need your payroll records. Files you send on the start page, or by email, are kept in a private store at our database provider, Supabase, that only we can read. We use them only to prepare and file your certified payroll, and we delete them on request to support@easywh347.com. We keep the contact details you give us (email, company, phone) to reach you about the service.

What we see

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs: IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These contain no payroll data.
  • We count visits and button presses with Plausible Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. The events we count contain no payroll data, only things like "PDF downloaded" and how many workers it had.
  • If you arrive by clicking one of our Google ads, Google's ad tag loads so we can tell whether the ad led to a downloaded payroll, including on return visits within 90 days of that click. It can set Google advertising cookies. If you arrive from one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta's pixel loads for the same purpose, on the same 90-day terms, and can set Meta cookies. It is told only that a page was viewed, a PDF was downloaded or a plan was bought, never anything from your payroll. Visitors who never came from an ad get neither. We don't sell any information.
  • If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address and what you bought, not your card details.
  • If you email us, we keep the email so we can reply.
