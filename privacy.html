Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Your payroll is never uploaded

Whether you type the payroll into the form or import a payroll file, it is handled by code running in your browser tab. The workers' names, ID numbers, hours and pay are not sent to us or to anyone else. Once the page has loaded you can switch off your internet connection and the tool still works, which is a simple way to confirm this yourself.

What your browser remembers

To save you typing next week, your browser keeps the report header (company, project and wage determination details), the crew you typed into the form (names, the last four digits of each ID, classifications, rates, deductions and hours), your column matching and any wage rates you pasted. Full Social Security numbers are never kept. This is stored in your browser's local storage on your computer only. "Clear the form" on the page, or clearing your browser data, removes it.

What we see

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs: IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These contain no payroll data.
  • We count visits and button presses with Plausible Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. The events we count contain no payroll data, only things like "PDF downloaded" and how many workers it had.
  • If you arrive by clicking one of our Google ads, Google's ad tag loads so we can tell whether the ad led to a downloaded payroll. It can set Google advertising cookies. Visitors who don't come from an ad don't get it. We don't sell any information.
  • If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address and what you bought, not your card details.
  • If you email us, we keep the email so we can reply.
