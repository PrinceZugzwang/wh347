Why DIR rejects eCPR files, and the fix

From DIR's eCPR XML Guidelines, version 2.

The file isn't well-formed XML
A tag is left open, or a character such as & or < in a name or address isn't escaped. Write &amp; for &. Open the file in a browser: it points at the first broken line.
Wrong format or version 1 file
A version 2 file starts with <CPR:eCPR> and its <CPR:version> says 2.0. Files for New York, LCPtracker or other systems use different tags and won't load in DIR.
Version isn't 2.0
Leave the version exactly as DIR's sample has it: 2.0.
Fields removed in version 2
These version 1 fields now make the upload fail: contractorLicense, contractorFEIN, contractorAddress, insuranceNum, contractorEmail, contractAgencyID, contractAgency, projectName, awardingBodyID, projectNum, contractID and projectLocation. Delete them. Older payroll software still writes some of them.
Registration number (PWCR)
Exactly the 10 digits of your public works contractor registration, and it has to match what DIR has on file. "NA" is no longer accepted. An expired registration also fails: renew it on DIR's site first.
Project ID
The numeric project ID DIR gave the project when the awarding body filed the PWC-100. Not the agency's contract number. Ask the prime or the awarding body if you don't have it.
Contractor name
Required, up to 56 characters.
Weeks with no work
For a week with no work on the project, set statementOfNP to true (lower case) and leave out the employees. Submitted payrolls can't be deleted, so a non-performance statement is also how you cover a week you skipped.
Dates
Dates are written YYYY-MM-DD. Each classification has seven days in order: day 1 is six days before the week-ending date and day 7 is the week-ending date itself.
Seven days, numbered 1 to 7
Every classification needs exactly seven <day> elements with id 1 to 7, even for days off (enter 0.0).
Hours
Each day's straight time, overtime and double time is a number under 100 with up to two decimals. A worker can't have more than 24 hours on one date across all classifications, and can't have zero hours for the week.
Weekly totals don't match the days
The three weekly totals must equal the sum of the daily hours, to the hundredth.
Fields out of order
DIR reads fields in a fixed order and fails the file even when every value is present. For each employee: firstName, lastName, middleName, address, ssn, itin, notes, classifications, payments, deductions, earnings.
Names
First and last name are required, up to 56 characters each.
Address
Street (up to 100 characters), city, a two-letter state in capitals, and a ZIP as 12345 or 12345-6789.
SSN or ITIN
At least one, 9 digits with no dashes or spaces. The same SSN can't appear twice in one file.
Classification and craft
Every worker needs at least one classification with a name and a craft. Use the official names from the wage determination.
Journeyman or apprentice
employeeLevel is journeyman or apprentice, in lower case. Apprentices need an apprentice level from 1 to 10; journeymen leave it empty.
Fringe
All five fringe amounts must be present; enter 0.0 where one doesn't apply. At least one of paidToEmployee and paidToBenefitPlan must be true, even when no fringe is owed.
Pay rates
All six rate fields are required and under 10,000. If there are straight-time hours, the straight-time rate can't be 0.
Deductions
All eight deduction fields are required (0.0 if none), and totalDeductions must equal their sum. Travel and subsistence go under payments, not deductions, and fringe never counts as a deduction.
Earnings and check number
Gross for this project, gross for all work, net pay and a check number (up to 20 characters; write CASH for cash). DIR stores these as entered and does not recheck them, so a wrong figure goes through silently.
Too long
Limits: names and awarding body 56 characters, street 100, city 250, notes 256, check number 20.
Payroll and amendment numbers
Leave payrollNum and amendmentNum empty. DIR assigns them.
More than 500 workers
DIR takes up to 500 employees per file. Split the week into two files.
Missing sections
A file needs contractorInfo, projectInfo and payrollInfo, in that order.

When the portal itself is the problem

  • The XML loads but nothing appears. DIR's FAQ acknowledges reports of this and offers no fix. Try another browser, or enter the week in the web form.
  • The web form doesn't save. Finish in one sitting, or submit what you have and send an amended payroll for the rest.
  • You made a mistake. Resubmit the same week; the amended version replaces the original.
  • The prime said LCPtracker is enough. It isn't. DIR requires its own submission from every contractor, even when you also file in LCPtracker.
