EasyWH347

Certified payroll Excel template

A free spreadsheet for your weekly Davis-Bacon certified payroll, laid out like Form WH-347. Type in the week; totals, overtime past 40 hours, gross and net pay are worked out for you.

or fill in the WH-347 online instead

Turn it into the WH-347

When the week is filled in, drop the file on easywh347.com. It opens in the online form, checks each worker's pay and gives you the WH-347 as a PDF in the current January 2025 layout, with the Department of Labor's own signing page. The file is read in your browser and isn't uploaded.

What goes in each column

Report header
Your company and address, the project, its location and contract number, the wage determination number, the payroll number and the Saturday that ends the week.
Name, last 4 of SSN
One row per worker. Only the last four digits of the Social Security number, which is all the form shows.
Classification
The trade exactly as written on the wage determination, for example Electrician or Laborer, Group 2.
J or RA
J for a journeyworker, RA for a registered apprentice, with the apprentice's level or period beside it.
Sun to Sat
Hours worked on this project each day.
Rate and fringe
The basic hourly rate, the hourly amount paid into fringe benefit plans, and any fringe paid to the worker in cash.
Deductions
FICA, federal and state tax withheld and other deductions for the week. Add gross for all work only if the worker also earned pay off this project.
Shaded columns
Total hours, overtime past 40, gross on this project and net pay. These are formulas; leave them alone.

What a spreadsheet can't check

  • Whether each rate and fringe meets your wage determination. The online form checks this when you paste the rates in.
  • Daily overtime rules some states add on state-funded work. The template follows the federal rule: hours past 40 in the week.
  • The Statement of Compliance. It has to be the Department of Labor's own wording, signed by the certifying official.

Keep every week

Certified payrolls must be kept for three years after all the work on the prime contract is finished, together with the time records and pay records behind them. Saving each week's workbook alongside the PDF is a simple way to do that.
